2 · What we never publish

  • Any customer's name, face, message, email, phone number or payment.

  • Your logins, or anything inside your accounts beyond the numbers you ticked.
  • Anything you did not say yes to.

3 · How your numbers are read

  • You read them from your own tools, or we read them inside your accounts while you watch.
  • The "before" numbers come from the four weeks before the setup starts, read from your tools' own history. Your hours, which no tool records, are your estimate, and we publish them as an estimate.
  • The "after" numbers come from the weeks after it is handed over, for up to three months.
  • We copy only the numbers you ticked. We keep no copy of your customers' data.

4 · Your yes on every piece

  • Before anything that shows your business goes out, we send it to you.
  • We publish only after your written yes. No answer means we do not publish.
  • You may ask for changes, or say no to any piece, without giving a reason.

5 · Changing your mind

  • You can withdraw this consent at any time, by email or message, without giving a reason.
  • Within 7 days we take down every post on our own accounts that shows your business or your numbers, and we publish nothing new.
  • We cannot recall copies others have already shared or saved.
  • Withdrawing costs you nothing. What is already set up in your accounts stays yours. You can also withdraw only your name, your words, your video or the screens. Then the posts that show them come down within 7 days, anything published after that shows your business by type and country only, and your setup goes on. If you withdraw the whole consent before the setup ends, we stop at the end of that week and hand over what is built.

6 · What we keep

  • This form and the numbers read from your accounts, for as long as anything about your business is published, and for 90 days after it comes down. Your application, until 90 days after your setup ends.
  • What we learn may shape our future work, with no name, number or detail that identifies you.
